length, bill depth, flipper length, body mass, sex, and year. The
dataset includes three penguin species: Adelie, Chinstrap, and Gentoo,
which are observed on three islands: Biscoe, Dream, and Torgersen. Also,
the dataset contains 344 rows and 8 columns.The mean flipper length is
200.92 mm.
