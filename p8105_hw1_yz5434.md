The mean works for the numeric and logical variables, but not for the
character or factor variables.

``` r
as.numeric(pull(q2_df, log_vec))
as.numeric(pull(q2_df, char_vec))
as.numeric(pull(q2_df, fac_vec))
```

`as.numeric()` converts logical variable to 1s and 0s, where `TRUE` is 1
and `FALSE` is 0. The character variable is converted to `NA`. The
factor variable is converted to 1, 2, and 3 respectively based on its
underlying integer codes (A is 1, B is 2, and C is 3). This helps
explain why `mean()` works for logical variables but not directly for
character or factor variables.
